# Research agent

**Use for**: keyword expansion, competitor scan, and audience framing before launching a new theme or expanding to a new city.

## Prompt template

```
I'm running Google Search Ads for FirstHomeBayArea, a tool that helps first-time
Bay Area buyers evaluate condos (HOA health, true monthly cost, comparable sales)
rather than just browse listings like Zillow/Redfin.

Current market: [CITY]. Current keyword theme: [THEME NAME AND INTENT, e.g.
"calculation intent - buyer trying to figure out what they can afford"].

Give me:
1. 10 additional keyword candidates matching this intent, with your best guess at
   relative search volume and commercial intent (low/med/high) for each.
2. 3 competitors likely bidding on this space in [CITY], and what their landing
   page / offer probably looks like (search their site if you can).
3. One audience insight I might be missing — a sub-segment of "first-time buyer"
   with a distinct need I haven't targeted yet.

Do not invent search volume numbers as if they were verified data — flag anything
you're estimating versus anything you found from an actual source.
```
